I would not approve the revised specification yet.

1. **[P1] Point-in-mesh still lacks reliable boundary and inconclusive semantics.**
   The parity rules only reject hits near triangle edges or vertices ([spec:401](/Users/kangea/Documents/Workspace/Codes/aps-viewer-clash-detection/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-06-collision-library-milestone1-design.md:401)). A sample starting on the interior of a target face can produce a distance-zero hit—raycasting defaults to `near = 0`—and be counted as inside depending on ray direction. Pure face contact can therefore become `volume-overlap`. Additionally, the test expects a shared-edge hit to count once ([spec:654](/Users/kangea/Documents/Workspace/Codes/aps-viewer-clash-detection/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-06-collision-library-milestone1-design.md:654)), while the algorithm declares every edge hit inconclusive. [`MeshBVH.raycast` contract](https://github.com/gkjohnson/three-mesh-bvh/blob/master/API.md#raycast). Define an explicit `inside | outside | boundary/inconclusive` result, detect surface proximity, and propagate inconclusive candidate tests through `ProperCrossingQuery`.

2. **[P1] `interiorOffset` introduces an undeclared minimum penetration depth.**
   `ProperCrossingQuery` always moves `1e-4 × localBoundsDiagonal` away from the intersection curve ([spec:296](/Users/kangea/Documents/Workspace/Codes/aps-viewer-clash-detection/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-06-collision-library-milestone1-design.md:296), [spec:338](/Users/kangea/Documents/Workspace/Codes/aps-viewer-clash-detection/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-06-collision-library-milestone1-design.md:338)). A positive overlap thinner than that displacement can have every candidate step completely through the overlap. Perpendicular thin boxes can then have no vertex-containment witness either, producing a clean false negative. Use adaptive offsets or explicitly define `volume-overlap` as requiring a minimum penetration tolerance. The spec must also identify whose local diagonal controls the offset to preserve symmetry.

3. **[P1] Coplanar triangle pairs do not provide the intersection segment the design assumes.**
   The design unconditionally takes the midpoint returned by `ExtendedTriangle.intersectsTriangle(other, targetLine)` ([spec:336](/Users/kangea/Documents/Workspace/Codes/aps-viewer-clash-detection/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-06-collision-library-milestone1-design.md:336)). The upstream implementation’s coplanar branch warns and sets the target edge to zero because no unique segment is produced; consequently, the argument that candidates lie on opposite sides of the actual contact plane is invalid. See upstream [`ExtendedTriangle`](https://github.com/gkjohnson/three-mesh-bvh/blob/master/src/math/ExtendedTriangle.js). Coplanar overlap needs a separate classification path. The exact compatible `three` and `three-mesh-bvh` versions should also be stated rather than merely described as “pinned.”

4. **[P1] The connected-component exactness proof contradicts the acknowledged under-segmentation case.**
   Unioning triangles that share any vertex can combine otherwise separate solids ([spec:305](/Users/kangea/Documents/Workspace/Codes/aps-viewer-clash-detection/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-06-collision-library-milestone1-design.md:305)). The claim that their self-contact means the A-vs-B contact query decides the result ([spec:313](/Users/kangea/Documents/Workspace/Codes/aps-viewer-clash-detection/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-06-collision-library-milestone1-design.md:313)) is incorrect. Two solids can share one vertex on B’s boundary, with one solid inside B and the other outside; there is no transverse A/B crossing, yet four representatives from the combined component can miss the contained solid. Either use a stronger component definition, require manifold inputs, or remove the “exact” guarantee and diagnose under-segmented cases.

5. **[P2] Model removal invalidates only room IDs, not model-scoped collision data.**
   Geometry records and BLASes are keyed by `(modelId, geometryIndex)` ([spec:249](/Users/kangea/Documents/Workspace/Codes/aps-viewer-clash-detection/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-06-collision-library-milestone1-design.md:249)), but the model-removal path only clears the room cache ([spec:459](/Users/kangea/Documents/Workspace/Codes/aps-viewer-clash-detection/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-06-collision-library-milestone1-design.md:459)); collision records are explicitly freed only by `world.dispose()` ([spec:521](/Users/kangea/Documents/Workspace/Codes/aps-viewer-clash-detection/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-06-collision-library-milestone1-design.md:521)). This leaks unloaded-model geometry and risks stale BLAS reuse if model IDs are recycled. Model removal should evict that model’s instances, reference counts, geometry records, pending builds, and BLASes.

The `model.search` correction, role-aware diagnostics, tolerance-unit split, and removal of `firstHitOnly` are satisfactory.
